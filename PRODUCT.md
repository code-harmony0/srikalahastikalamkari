# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Shoppers across India looking for authentic hand-painted Srikalahasti Kalamkari: mostly women buying sarees and dupattas for themselves, family or occasions, plus buyers of wall paintings, fabrics and custom commissions. Many arrive on mobile from Instagram or WhatsApp.

The shop owner and their team run the catalogue, orders, coupons and inquiries from the admin dashboard.

## Product Purpose

An online store for Srikalahasti Kalamkari pieces made in Srikalahasti village, Andhra Pradesh. Success is a visitor trusting that the craft is real, finding the right category quickly, and buying or starting a custom order.

## Positioning

Pen (kalam) Kalamkari hand-painted in Srikalahasti itself, a GI-registered craft, sold directly by the makers rather than a reseller catalogue.

## Operating Context

- Customers browse by category (Sarees and their fabrics, Pure Silk Sarees, Dupattas, Fabrics, Wall Paintings, Customisations, Men's wear) and contact the shop on WhatsApp.
- Custom designs are discussed on WhatsApp before any commitment.
- The client's marketing team reviews the site and sends reference sites.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Payment: Razorpay only (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets). No cash on delivery.
- Shipping: India only, free shipping, prices include all taxes.
- No returns, refunds or cancellations. All sales are final.
- Dispatch time is not fixed; the site must not promise one. Made-to-order pieces are the exception, with their stated lead time.
- No gift wrapping.
- Category counts, prices and stock come from the live database and change often.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Srikalahasti Kalamkari. Logo: gold crest with crown around a royal-blue roundel carrying the Telugu "Sri" letter.
- Theme (client decision, 2026-09-15, revised same day): "Sky & Gold", taken from the logo. Cool near-white pages, soft sky-blue bands, deep navy text (no brown or black text), crest gold for frames, rules and ornament only (never text or fills behind text on light grounds), midnight blue for the announcement bar, dark bands and footer. Must not return to the earlier cream/brown-black look.
- Colour roles (owner decision 2026-09-15): primary buttons are midnight navy #0B2A55 (hover deeper + thin gold line); secondary buttons are white with a navy outline; logo royal blue #0A63B8 is reserved for text links, selected states and focus rings (~3% of a page); icons are navy ink; headings carry no coloured words. Hover never turns an element gold on a light background.
- Readability is a client requirement: every text colour must pass WCAG AA (4.5:1) on its actual background; no faded (low-opacity) text for content.
- Home page is content-led, telling the craft story, not an aggressive sales page (client marketing team, 2026-09-15).
- Numbers and prices are always set in plain sans, never a decorative serif.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: product photos and catalogue in Supabase, category images for most categories, the logo (`public/images/srikalahasti-logo.jpg`), GSTIN 37EVOPS6191D1ZX, GI registration, store addresses and WhatsApp number in the footer.
- Absent, must not be fabricated: customer reviews or testimonials (none collected yet), artisan-family counts, years-of-heritage figures, sales counts, "best seller" data.

## Product Principles

1. Show the craft truthfully; never invent reviews, counts or claims.
2. Let categories do the navigation work; a visitor should reach the right fabric in one tap.
3. Keep the story, but keep it short and concrete.
4. Match the logo: the brand should feel like the crest, not a generic luxury store.
